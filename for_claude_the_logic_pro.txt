the more likely explanations are (a) MobileFPS' own Update-driven movement not actually being gated by
Time.timeScale at all (CharacterController.Move uses Time.deltaTime, and deltaTime is itself scaled by
timeScale in Unity's normal Update, BUT Time.deltaTime is only scaled by timeScale for the FIXED-time-step path;

looking at the wrong Time.timeScale (e.g. a stale IL2CPP domain), or (c) something is calling Time.timeScale = 1
right after the mod sets it to 0 THIS SAME FRAME via one of the Escape/pause paths above if PlayerGate's read of
Paused.IsPaused disagrees with the game's own Paused.IsPaused at that instant. Worth checking: does the mod pause
via Time.timeScale directly, and is Paused.IsPaused ever true/false in a way that makes Paused::Update's Esc
branch fire unexpectedly (e.g. injected Escape presses)?

[[IDA:Inventory_DropLogic_0x1802454d0]]
