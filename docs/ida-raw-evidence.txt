VERDICT: MobileFPS.FallSpeed does not accumulate at all. It is a compile-time constant (9.81,
assigned once in the constructor) used as a flat downward speed, multiplied by Time.deltaTime
exactly once per frame at every consumption site (HandleVerticalMovement and the inlined duplicate
in Update). There is no `FallSpeed += gravity * dt` integration anywhere in the binary -- the
player's fall speed is identical on the first frame of a fall and the 1000th frame of the same
fall (no ramp-up). This is LINEAR IN DT (motion = 9.81 * down * dt, single multiply, not dt^2) but
NOT an accumulator -- there is nothing to accumulate. Existing findings on frame-rate-independent
fall distance-per-second stand; the only residual frame-rate sensitivity remains the discrete
CharacterController.Move call count during a fall (collision-resolution granularity), unrelated to
FallSpeed's own (nonexistent) integration.
